FAQ

Do you build with PHP forms?

Yes. For simple marketing sites, a PHP form can send inquiries to the chosen support email while keeping communication form-based.

Can you add analytics?

We can prepare events and GTM/GA4 readiness so tracking can be configured around real actions.

Can you maintain it after launch?

Yes. Support and iteration can cover fixes, content changes, campaign landing pages and performance improvements.
