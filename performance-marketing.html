Where this helps

Performance marketing works when the whole path after the click makes sense.

We do not treat media buying as a separate activity. Campaign structure, creative testing, landing page clarity, tracking and follow-up are planned together, because weak pages and unclear offers waste budget just as quickly as poor targeting.

Example: a company is spending on Meta and Google but cannot tell which leads are useful. We rebuild the campaign structure, align ads with landing pages, add conversion events and report on inquiry quality instead of vanity metrics.

FAQ

Do you only run ads?

No. Marmex connects ads to landing pages, analytics, creative production and follow-up systems.

Can you work with existing accounts?

Yes. We can audit and improve existing Google Ads, Meta, TikTok or LinkedIn structures before rebuilding anything.

What should be ready before launch?

At minimum: a clear offer, a landing page or service page, working form, tracking access and a realistic test budget.
